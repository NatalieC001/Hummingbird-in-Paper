# CB09 Knife System - Blades & Holders for 180gsm Craft Paper

## CB09 Compatibility & Lifespan

The CB09 system is a professional blade holder system compatible with most vinyl cutters (Cricut Maker, Silhouette Cameo, etc. with appropriate adapter).

---

## Blades for 180gsm Craft Paper

**30° angle** - Standard cutting, best for 180gsm
- Lifespan: 50-100 cuts

**45° angle** - Steeper, for thicker materials (200-300gsm)
- Lifespan: 30-60 cuts

**60° angle** - Very steep, for heavy cardstock
- Lifespan: 15-30 cuts

**Recommendation for 180gsm craft paper:** Use 30° or 45° blades

---

## Long-Life Blade Options (NOT plastic crap)

**Tungsten Carbide** ✓
5-10x longer life than steel. Lasts 300-500+ cuts on 180gsm paper. More expensive but worth it.

**Ceramic/Titanium coated** ✓
3-4x standard steel life. Resists dulling from paper fibers.

**Standard steel** ✗
Avoid for longevity. Dulls after 50-100 cuts.
