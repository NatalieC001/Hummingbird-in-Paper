## Search Steps for Archilochus [portal.idigbio.org](https://portal.idigbio.org/portal/search)
Open [iDigBio Search](https://portal.idigbio.org/portal/search).

Taxonomy: Add Genus → Type Archilochus.

Photos: Check the box "Must have media".

Specimen Type: Add Preparations → Type skin.

View: Click the Media tab at the top to see the image gallery.

## Filter Keywords
Use these in the Preparations or Search all fields box:

study skin

flat skin

round skin

## Quick Tip
The Media tab is the fastest way to browse photos without reading through rows of text data.

## Key Terms for Your Search
search for a genus (e.g., Archilochus). Filter for "Media" or "Images" to find records with photos.

"Study Skin": "flat skins" or "round skins".

"Dorsal/Ventral": Use these to see the back vs. the belly.

"Contour Feathers": the small body feathers missing on [Featherbase](https://www.featherbase.info/).

"Plumage": for variations (winter vs. summer), search for "Non-breeding" or "Alternate" plumage.
