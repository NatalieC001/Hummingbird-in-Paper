## Search: *Archilochus* on [iDigBio](https://portal.idigbio.org/portal/search)

**Steps:**
1. Taxonomy → Genus → `Archilochus`
2. Check "Must have media"
3. Preparations → Type `skin`
4. Click **Media tab** for gallery

**Filter keywords:** `study skin`, `flat skin`, `round skin`

**Key terms:** `Dorsal`/`Ventral` (back vs. belly), `Contour Feathers` (small body feathers missing on [Featherbase](https://www.featherbase.info/)), `Plumage` + `Non-breeding` or `Alternate` (seasonal variation)
