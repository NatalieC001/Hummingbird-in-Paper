# Feather library

## Common Birds and Songbirds

[Passeriformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/passeriformes) - **Common Birds and Songbirds**: Passeriformes includes finches, ravens, and magpies, representing over half of all bird species.

---

## Parrots and Tropical Birds

[Psittaciformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/psittaciformes) includes parrots, macaws, and cockatoos. [Musophagiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/musophagiformes) contains turacos, while [Trogoniformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/trogoniformes) includes the quetzal.

---

## Owls and Birds of Prey

[Strigiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/strigiformes) covers barn owls and typical owls. Diurnal raptors like eagles and hawks are found in [Accipitriformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/accipitriformes), while falcons belong to [Falconiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/falconiformes).

---

## Waterfowl and Marine Birds

[Anseriformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/anseriformes) includes ducks, geese, and swans. [Sphenisciformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/sphenisciformes) contains penguins. [Pelecaniformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/pelecaniformes) covers pelicans and herons. [Procellariiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/procellariiformes) includes albatrosses and petrels. [Gaviiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/gaviiformes) covers loons. [Podicipediformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/podicipediformes) includes grebes. [Phaethontiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/phaethontiformes) contains tropicbirds.

---

## Woodpeckers and Kingfishers

[Piciformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/piciformes) includes woodpeckers and toucans. [Coraciiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/coraciiformes) features kingfishers and bee-eaters.

---

## Large Flightless Birds

[Struthioniformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/struthioniformes) contains ostriches. [Casuariiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/casuariiformes) includes emus and cassowaries.

---

## Gamebirds, Waders, and Others

[Galliformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/galliformes) includes chickens and pheasants. [Charadriiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/charadriiformes) covers gulls and plovers. [Gruiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/gruiformes) includes cranes. [Columbiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/columbiformes) contains pigeons and doves. [Apodiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/apodiformes) includes swifts and hummingbirds. [Caprimulgiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/caprimulgiformes) covers nightjars. [Cuculiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/cuculiformes) includes cuckoos. [Phoenicopteriformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/phoenicopteriformes) contains flamingos. [Ciconiiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/ciconiiformes) includes storks. [Bucerotiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/bucerotiformes) covers hornbills and hoopoes. [Pteroclidiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/pteroclidiformes) contains sandgrouse. [Coliiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/coliiformes) includes mousebirds. [Leptosomiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/leptosomiformes) features the cuckoo roller. [Eurypygiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/eurypygiformes) includes the sunbittern. [Otidiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/otidiformes) covers bustards. [Cariamiformes](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/cariamiformes) includes seriemas. [Opisthocomiformes](https://www.featherbase.info/id/order/opisthocomiformes) contains the hoatzin.
