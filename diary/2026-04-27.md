## Notes

### 1. Optimal Tolerances

- **Press Fit:** **0.15mm - 0.2mm**. Best for parts that stay together via friction.
    
- **Loose/Glue Fit:** **0.3mm - 0.5mm**. Best for parts requiring adhesive or if the filament tends to swell.
    
- **A1 Mini Precision:** This printer handles tight tolerances well, but always chamfer (slope) the edges of your keys to help them "lead in" to the slot.
    

### 2. Orientation & Strength

- **The 45° Rule:** Rotating parts at 45° often provides the best compromise between detail and strength, as it prevents shear force from being applied directly along a single layer line.
    
- **Layer Alignment:** Never print a thin key standing straight up (Z-axis); it will snap like a toothpick. Print it horizontally or at an angle so the layers run the length of the part.
