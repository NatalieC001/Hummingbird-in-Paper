## Notes

### 1. Optimal Tolerances

- Press Fit: 0.15mm - 0.2mm. Best for parts that lock via friction.
    
- Loose/Glue Fit: 0.3mm - 0.5mm. Best for gluing or if filament swells.
    
- **A1 Mini Precision:** handles detail well, but always chamfer keys to help them fit.
    

### 2. Orientation & Strength

- **The 5° Rule:** Rotating parts prevents shear force along a single layer line.
    
- **Layer Alignment:** Never print a thin key standing straight up (Z-axis); it will snap like a toothpick. Print it horizontally or at an angle so the layers run the length of the part.
